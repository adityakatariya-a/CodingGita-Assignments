### 5. Divide and Assign `/=`

1. Total of 180 chocolates is shared among 6 children. Update using `/=`.

**Answer:**

```js
let chocolate = 180;
chocolate /= 6;
console.log(chocolate)
```

---

2. Distance of 300 km is covered in 5 hours. Find average speed using `/=`.

**Answer:**

```js
let distance = 300
distance /= 5
console.log("Avarage Speed :", distance)
```
